Inbox Triage

Inbox Triage is a personal tool that helps one person (its owner) get their Gmail inbox to zero. It runs on the owner's own home server and is not offered to anyone else.

Each day it groups inbox mail by sender and suggests an action: archive, add a label, mark as needing a reply, or unsubscribe. Nothing happens until the owner approves it. It never deletes or sends email, and every change can be undone.
